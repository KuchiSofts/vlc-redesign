# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome here: users, translators, designers, developers, whatever their experience, origin, identity
or language.

**Expected:** be kind and patient; assume good intent; explain and ask rather than demand; accept that others may
disagree; give credit; keep discussions about the software.

**Not accepted:** insults, harassment, discrimination, sexual language or imagery, publishing someone's private
information, or any conduct that would be out of place in a workplace.

**Scope:** issues, pull requests, discussions, the wiki, and any other space of the project, including when
representing it elsewhere.

**Reports:** write to the maintainer through the e-mail on the
[KuchiSofts profile](https://github.com/KuchiSofts), or, if the report concerns the maintainer, to GitHub's own
[report form](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse). Reports are read, kept private, and answered. Responses
range from a private word to removal from the project's spaces, as the maintainer judges fair.

This text follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1,
in fewer words.
